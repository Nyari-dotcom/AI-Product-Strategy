## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):** Popcorn & Pitch will increase the number of promising startups that secure funding by helping founders present stronger opportunities and helping investors identify relevant deals faster.

**The case:**
1. Why now: The market no longer has a shortage of startup content. Founders can already generate pitch decks, business plans, and research using widely available AI tools. The bottleneck is identifying credible opportunities and connecting them to the right investors. The opportunity is not content creation; it is reducing friction between idea validation, investor discovery, and funding progression.
2. What's defensible: Our defensibility is not the AI assessment itself. Contextual moat is currently 2/5, Data Advantage 3/5, and Platform Exposure 3/5. The long-term moat is a proprietary funding-outcome dataset connecting founder submissions, investor preferences, introductions, due-diligence progression, and eventual funding outcomes. Competitors can copy scoring features. They cannot easily replicate verified investor relationships and outcome data accumulated over time.
3. The economics: The business currently supports an estimated gross margin of 78.3%, with revenue of R299 per user against AI COGS of R65 per user. Even if inference costs triple, margin remains 58.2%. The economics are viable. The primary question is not cost efficiency. The primary question is whether we can consistently generate valuable investor introductions and funding outcomes.

**The risks:**
1. Trust / failure modes: The highest-risk failure is recommending poor-quality businesses as investment-ready or presenting unsupported market intelligence that influences investor decisions. The platform addresses this through confidence scoring, human-in-the-loop reviews, escalation thresholds, hallucination monitoring, and explicit governance where AI never makes investment decisions.
2. Scale / governance: At 10x scale, governance becomes harder than technology. Human review costs increase. Compliance complexity grows. Provider dependency remains a risk with a portability score of 2/5. The platform addresses this through model abstraction, multi-provider evaluation, governance agents, monthly benchmark testing, and structured escalation processes.
3. Competitive: The most credible threat is AngelList. They already have investor relationships, distribution, workflow integration, and trust. If Popcorn & Pitch cannot demonstrate that at least 10% of qualified founder-investor matches progress to serious funding discussions during a 90-day pilot, the product should be reconsidered or stopped.

**The ask:**
The strategy does not currently specify funding requirements, team composition, or budget. Before approval, leadership should require:
- Pilot budget allocation.
- Required headcount.
- 90-day validation timeline.
- Target founder and investor participation numbers.
- Success metrics tied to introductions, engagement, diligence progression, and funding outcomes.

The recommendation is to fund a focused validation phase and defer investment in non-differentiating content-generation features. Resources should be concentrated on proving investor matching quality and funding progression.
